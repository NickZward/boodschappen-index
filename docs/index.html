🛒 Boodschappenindex

An independent daily measurement of Dutch supermarket prices, tracking a fixed basket of 72 everyday products at Albert Heijn. Base date = 100.

Method: fixed basket (Laspeyres), category weights approximate CBS household spending shares. Shelf price = price before promotions; paid price = including bonus discounts; unit price = price per liter/kilo (this one also moves on shrinkflation). One daily snapshot of a single retailer; this is not an official inflation measure. Data and code: github.com/NickZward/boodschappen-index.
